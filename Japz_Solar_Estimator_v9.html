Final design verification gate

The app will not label a design installation-ready. It checks entered data and flags evidence still needed; site inspection, code interpretation, utility permission and final electrical sign-off cannot be replaced by software.

Engineering notes & assumptions

  • Energy model: quantity × input watts × hours per day ÷ 1,000. It does not model appliance duty cycles unless reflected in daily hours.
  • PV energy estimate = array kWp × peak sun hours × performance ratio (default 0.80).
  • Battery runtime uses usable battery energy × inverter efficiency divided by the modeled load. Real performance varies.
  • Final design must use manufacturer datasheets for Voc temperature correction, Isc, MPPT range, input current, battery limits, conductor sizing and protection coordination.
  • Grid-tied equipment may shut down during outages unless it has an approved backup output and correct transfer/isolation arrangement.
  • The app deliberately blocks an installation-ready label. A qualified electrical professional and relevant authority/utility must verify the site-specific design and applicable Philippine requirements before installation.
